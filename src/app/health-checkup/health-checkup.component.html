Preventive Health Screenings for a Healthier Tomorrow

At MIMS Hospital, our full body health check-up packages and executive health screening programmes are designed to meet the healthcare needs of individuals across different age groups and risk profiles.

  • Same-day sample collection with a 24×7 clinical laboratory.
  • Advanced imaging: MRI, 16-slice CT, ultrasound, mammography and digital X-ray.
  • Consultation with the relevant specialist to walk through your results.
  • Clear follow-up guidance and long-term management where needed.
