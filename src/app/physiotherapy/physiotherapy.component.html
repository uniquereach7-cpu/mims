Overview

Advanced Physiotherapy and Rehabilitation Treatment for Faster Recovery

The Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Department at MIMS Hospital focuses on restoring mobility, improving physical function, reducing pain, and helping individuals return to their daily activities with confidence.

Our team provides post-surgical rehabilitation programmes, sports injury rehabilitation treatment, and neurological rehabilitation services tailored to individual recovery goals. Working closely with specialists across multiple departments, our physiotherapists deliver evidence-based rehabilitation programmes designed to support faster recovery, improved function, and long-term well-being.
