Inspired by a Visionary Leader — Dr. A. Murthy Raju

The foundation of MIMS reflects the vision and dedication of founder Dr. Alluri Murthy Raju, an eminent industrialist, educationist, philanthropist, and visionary leader. Under his guidance, Sri Rama Educational Trust established MIMS with the goal of creating a world-class institution that combines exceptional medical education with accessible, patient-centred healthcare.

Today, MIMS continues to build on this legacy by investing in modern infrastructure, advanced medical technology, clinical excellence, and community service — strengthening its position as a trusted healthcare and medical education destination in Andhra Pradesh.
